Homey Pro app · Open source

Shopping Companion

Shopping lists, price & stock monitoring and parcel events — stored on your own Homey.

🔒 No checkout. No payments. No Amazon password. No order placement.
Setup & support View on GitHub See features

Shopping lists

Create lists, add Amazon products, change quantities and remove items from Homey or a future ChatGPT connector.

Price watching

Trigger flows when a price changes, drops by a currency amount or falls by a chosen percentage.

Stock watching

React when a product goes out of stock, becomes unavailable or comes back.

Parcel events

Store tracking numbers and trigger flows on new parcels, status changes and delivery.

Multiple countries

Designed for Amazon marketplaces such as Sweden, Germany, UK, USA and more, with local currencies.

Private by design

Your lists and tracking data live on your Homey Pro. The app never stores an authenticated Amazon browser session.

No Creson backend

Your Homey is the backend. Creson does not need to host a product proxy or database for the app to work.

Two setup levels

BASIC — install the app, choose your marketplace, use lists and Flows LIVE DATA — optionally add your own Amazon Creators API credentials in Homey All Amazon API calls go directly from your Homey to Amazon

Built for Homey Flows

WHEN price drops ≥ 15% AND product is in stock THEN send me a notification
WHEN a new tracking number appears THEN pass it to my parcel workflow

Purchases always stay manual

Shopping Companion intentionally has no API for cart management, checkout, payment methods, shipping-address changes or placing an order. When you want to buy something, you open Amazon and complete the purchase yourself.
